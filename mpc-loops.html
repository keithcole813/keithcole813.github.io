Export

The loop WAV is exactly N bars at the locked BPM — drop it in the MPC, zone it, and every pad lands perfect. Slices export the same loop pre-cut into equal pads (4 / 8 / 16). One-shot mode exports your in/out region trimmed, with the dead air before the first sound removed.
How the 1 is found: leading silence is trimmed by the gate, the beat grid is phase-aligned to the strongest hits, and the first full bar from there is bar 1. Sparse material — a lone bassline, horns with no drums — can fool any detector: the BPM box, ÷2/×2, and the nudge buttons are there so your ear stays the final judge. Use the ruler-drag to slide the start close, then drag the gold In/Out bars on the wave — snapped to the grid, or free with snap off — to place it exactly.

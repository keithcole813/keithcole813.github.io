What is this? How do I use it?

The story: On my controller I cook by loading beats on different channels, syncing them, looping pieces at different sizes, splitting stems and riding the faders — long sets where my own beats turn into versions nobody's heard before. My dream rig is a DJM mixer in the middle with SP-404s and PLX-CRSS12 turntables on the sides. The Cook-Up Rig is that table, cloned as software: it looks like the hardware, floats on black, and every knob, fader and platter is real and moves.

How it works: The wide screen up top is my Serato-style window — waveforms with bar ticks so I can see everything line up. The mixer keeps it simple: gain up top (that's the real volume — faders play at 100%), EQ, level meters, one global effect, and reverb + delay at the master so the whole rig can sound like a nightclub. Faders start at the bottom, the crossfader lives in the middle, and the last 16 bars of every beat loop by default — no dead air, ever.

Feedback? Catch me live — the Rig gets its real test on the Tuesday Cook-Up, Tuesdays 8–10 PM ET on YouTube.

On the table

  • Two CRSS12 turntables — a loaded deck wears a real record: grooves, light rolling across it, and a label printed with the beat's name and a black marker line so you can see your place as it spins. Brake stops, backspins by dragging the record, and a reverse button
  • Sync that holds the set together — first deck playing becomes the Sync Master; followers tempo-match, Jump to Sync re-locks a deck in phase, Beat Jump hops 1–32 beats on the grid
  • Automatic cues — Cue 1 at the first sound (usually 00:00), Cue 2 at the first kick, set the moment a beat loads; tap to jump, make your own, they save per beat
  • Auto loop buttons — ½ to 32 beats, resize a running loop without breaking phase; plus the SAFETY 16 failsafe looping the last 16 bars by default
  • Auto gain — gains start at 50%, and when a new beat loads the gain knob turns itself to match the track that's playing. Even volume, no clip surprises
  • Master space — reverb and delay at the master with wet/dry mix, and a one-tap CLUB preset when the whole rig needs to sound like a nightclub
  • Coming in later betas — the SP-404 stem deck (dedicated stem pads and per-stem effects), real-time stem splitting, crates, and the rig growing to 4 channels as you add gear

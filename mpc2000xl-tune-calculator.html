“Set seq to” is the move you made tonight: take the clean key, then move the sequence to meet the sample instead of bending the sample between keys. Triggering pads per bar or beat resets the few-ms drift every pass.
Drift is how far the loop end lands from your bar line each pass — closest to 0 wins. If the sample's listed BPM is rounded, the true match can sit a click or two off; the table shows the neighbors so you can judge the trade.

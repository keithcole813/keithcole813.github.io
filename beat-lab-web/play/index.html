Song — Playlist

MPC2000XL style: each Sequence (above) is one bar of steps + notes. A Song strings sequences together — add them in order, set how many bars each one plays, and the transport runs the chain instead of looping a single sequence. Edit a sequence any time; the playlist just points at it. Using the same SEQ in several steps reuses that one sequence — an edit shows in every step that uses it. For a variation, use ⧉ Duplicate in the rack instead: the copy is fully independent, so changing it never touches the original.
Pattern mode — SEQ 1 loops on its own.
While the song plays, Follow jumps the rack view to each sequence as it’s reached — turn it off to edit a different sequence without the view moving. In Song mode, Export renders the whole playlist chain.

    Layout — Sequences + Linear Audio · one timeline

    Build both ways, under one playhead. The top lane is the sequence chain from the playlist; under it sit channel wave lanes and linear audio tracks. Drop a full vocal acapella (or any long audio) onto an audio track and it plays start-to-finish while your sequences run underneath — that’s the quick-remix lane. Click a linear clip to open the Linear Clip panel below the lanes: Find Tempo detects its BPM, Match project to clip makes the beat follow the vocal, Stretch clip to project time-stretches the vocal pitch-preserved onto your grid, edges can trim or stretch, and you can split a long clip into verse / hook pieces and rearrange them. Channel waves are not locked to the step grid: drag to shift, turn Snap off for free timing, nudge by the millisecond. A channel clip plays through its channel’s gain, fader and effects, and lands inside that channel’s stem; each audio track exports as its own stem.

    Piano Roll — 808

    No note selected — click a note block to select it.
    FL-style editing: click an empty cell to add a note · drag a note to move it (up/down = pitch, auditioned as you drag on pitched channels) · drag its right edge to change its length · right-click or long-press a note to delete it. Drum channels get the same treatment — their step-grid hits show as dashed blocks and become full notes the moment you drag, resize or set their velocity. In-key rows are tinted, roots are gold, out-of-key notes flag red on the 808.

    Stretch & Fit — tweak it while the beat plays

    Load a sample — Fit snaps its length to whole bars at the project BPM, MPC-style: speed and pitch move together.
    The pad way: hit the channel (rack chip, keys 1–9, your MPK) to fire it over the drums, drag Stretch or Pitch between hits and confirm by ear — a hit that’s still sounding bends live, and the next hit + the pattern use the new setting. Nothing stops the transport.

    Root note (pitched channels)

    Piano roll pitches the sound from this root. 808 root is set by “Tune 808” in Key Finder.

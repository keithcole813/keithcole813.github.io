6LOUD — Privacy Policy

6LOUD is a browser game published by Ninja Music Records. This page says plainly what the game stores, what it sends, and what it never does.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress in your browser's local storage, on your device only: your note balance, runners and upgrades you own, level unlocks, your local scores and best distances, your ghost-run recordings, your initials (if you enter them), and your settings. Clearing your browser data clears all of it. The game has no accounts and no sign-up.

What is sent — only when you tap PUBLISH

After a run, you may choose to publish your score to the world leaderboard. Only then does the game send your initials, your distance, and the replay recording of that run to our leaderboard database (hosted on Supabase). Nothing is sent automatically. Published scores and replays are visible to other players — that is what a leaderboard is.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where the law allows. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage ad personalization in your Google account settings.

What we never do

  • We never sell personal data.
  • We never collect your name, email address, or location through the game.
  • We never read anything outside the game's own storage.

Children

The game is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only text a player can publish is the initials they type for the leaderboard.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Every world in 6loud is its own place — its own sky, its own weather, and its own floor far below the clouds, graded from real photographs. These are real renders from the game itself.

The worlds

One endless course, eight worlds over it. Finish the distance in one world and the next unlocks — the run never stops, the world changes around you.

1DAY BREAK

Floor — the desert range

The Journey sky runs its whole arc — sunrise to noon to a moonless night — over a desert mountain range graded from real flight photos.

Where every run begins.

2GOLDEN HOUR

Floor — the city below

Six to nine in the evening, forever: the whole course floats over a dusk city in the haze.

Unlock — 5,000 FT in Day Break.

3MIDNIGHT RUN

Floor — the open ocean

Deep night, shooting stars, a real moon — and open ocean glittering far below the course.

Unlock — 8,000 FT in Golden Hour.

4ABOVE THE CLOUDS

Floor — the cloud sea

The storm world: rain decks, thunder clouds, seesaws and shiver clouds — over an unbroken sea of cloud.

Unlock — 13,000 FT in Midnight Run.

5FLOWER FIELD

Floor — the sunflower fields

Giant golden notes stand in a sunflower field under a sunset sky, and a single rainbow rides far behind it all.

Unlock — 15,000 FT in Above the Clouds.

6OVERTIME

Floor — range by day, ocean by night

One shift, two worlds: Day Break's course runs its 5,000 feet, then the light goes out and Midnight Run takes over — moon, stars and ocean.

Unlock — 8,000 FT in Flower Field.

7SUN SHOWER

Floor — the rolling plains

Storm rain first — then the same golden hour as Golden Hour, raining gold over rolling plains.

Unlock — 9,000 FT in Overtime.

8NIGHT SHIFT

Floor — the snow-capped ridge

The last shift: Midnight Run's night over a snow-capped ridge, handing over to Day Break's course before dawn.

Unlock — 10,000 FT in Sun Shower. Clear it and every world is open.

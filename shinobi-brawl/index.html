01 The Story

The streets run on somebody else's music now. A shadow promoter has bought up every stage, every club door and every patty shop corner from the Gaslamp to St. Croix — and the city's own sound is getting squeezed out. LaShmoove (the producer, MPC on his back, sword on his hip) and 6lade Cypher (the faster blade, a real black belt) fight a five-run war to take the stages back — brawling through waves of thugs in the streets, then facing the boss of every territory in a one-on-one duel.

03 How It Plays

Story Mode is a Streets-of-Rage-style brawler that keeps stopping for Street Fighter boss duels. VS Ladder is pure duels — eight rungs, ending with the three territory bosses. Both run on the same combat system:

🔥 CHARGE — fight on fire
Landing hits builds your charge meter; combos build it fastest. Spend it to catch fire: a glowing fighter deals bonus damage on every hit while the meter drains — combos keep it alive, but one clean hit knocks the fire out (you keep the meter). While glowing, your motion special becomes a screen-shaking SUPER.
🛡️ The blocking law
Blocking takes zero chip damage — but you must match the height. Block high for jump-ins and overheads, low for sweeps and crouch kicks. Block the wrong height and you eat it. Even weapons can be blocked — but block a blade too many times and it breaks until the next round.
💀 Finishers
On match point, the last hit decides how it ends: kill with an uppercut or a blade and it's a decapitation. Kill with a projectile and they fall limp. Anything else is a clean KO. The announcer (Keith himself) has opinions about all of it.
🐕 Companion dogs
Trap Diesel calls Zeus, a white albino Doberman in a camo service vest; Gangsta Slim's brown pitbull answers to him. Dogs attack on their own and can't be blocked — counter with a timed low kick. Kick one too often and it won't come back. A charged dog grabs and holds — wiggle the stick to break free before its owner tees off.
🚪 Stage knock-throughs
A heavy hit at the edge of an arena can smash your opponent through the wall into the next area — patty shop into the bakery, club doors into the club, rooftops down to the street. Health, charge and timer carry over. It's still the same fight, just a bigger stage.
🎁 The dojo cheat chest
After stage select, hold ↑ for 5 seconds on the matchup screen: chime, fade, and you're in the dojo facing one chest. Punch to stop the cycling icons and claim a cheat for 2 rounds — 1.5× life, speed, moon jumps, instant charge, invincibility, double jump, giant size, heavy hands. In a match, a button combo drops your cheat as a pickup… and yes, your opponent can steal it.

UNIT-13 is 13 feet of machine — too heavy to launch, and it doesn't wear damage like flesh does. Knock its head off below 25% health and it keeps fighting headless; the head stays on the floor and can be picked up and thrown. Its robot dog Spot (yellow, headless, backwards knees, laser on top) runs a six-second summon cycle and fires a tracking laser you cannot jump — the only answer is to block it. Beat them and UNIT-13 goes down the way machines should: it blows up.

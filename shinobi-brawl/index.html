01 — The Game

Four ways in

Street Fighter's duel, Mortal Kombat's endings, and a story told like a comic book you can hear. Every mode runs on the same combat system.

🥋

Arcade

The pure duel. Pick your fighter, pick your opponent, pick the stage — best of 3, winner stays sharp. The fastest way to a rematch.

1 v 1 · Your call
🪜

Ladder

Five rungs, each one meaner than the last, against the other finished fighters. Clear the ladder and the locked door at the top finally opens.

5 rungs · Unlocks Story

Story 🔒

A full campaign told in motion comics — static panels, slow zooms, one narrator. Its stages, its cast, and its ending stay classified. No ladder clear, no story.

Locked · Earn it
🥊

Practice

The lab. Set the dummy to stand, block, or take the hit — and yes, even the final boss is pickable here. Learn every super in private.

Training · No timer

🔥 Charge — fight on fire

Your hits build the meter. Chord L2 + R2 and you ignite: bonus damage on everything while the meter drains, until you chord off or burn out. Getting hit doesn't kill it anymore — the fire is yours until it's spent. At full glow, specials become SUPERS.

🛡️ The blocking law

Blocking takes zero chip damage — but you must match the height: stand for jump-ins, crouch for sweeps. Guess wrong and you eat it clean. Even blades can be blocked… but block a sword enough times in a round and it breaks.

💀 Finishers

Only the very last hit of a match can end it ugly. Kill with an uppercut or a blade — decapitation. Kill with a wave or a super — they drop limp. Kill by star or mic — they fall backwards, shot-style. The head that comes off can be kicked. It does no damage. It's the principle.

🚪 Knock-throughs

Certain walls are suggestions. A heavy hit at the edge smashes your opponent through the wall into the next area. Same fight, bigger stage, health and timer carried over.

04 — Story Mode

Story mode 🔒

Locked until you clear the Ladder. A full campaign told as motion comics — painted panels, slow zooms, a single voice narrating every run.

Five runs. One war for the city's stages. Somewhere in it, two of the most important locations sit a neighborhood apart — one of them is a story somebody told, and the other one actually happened. Which is which, who you meet on the road, and what waits at the end of it: all of it stays behind this door.

No screenshots. No location names. No cast list. The teaser declassifies when the mode does.

📱 Crate to Apple Music

Upload a Serato .crate file and every track gets cross-checked against Apple Music right here in your browser. Tap any result to open it. Songs with no confident match get flagged.

What is this? How do I use it?

The story: I DJ with Serato but work out with Apple Music on my phone. I wanted my DJ crates as phone-ready playlists without manually searching every track. This tool reads your Serato .crate file, looks up each song on Apple Music, and gives you the links — plus converts the audio to phone-friendly M4A if you want the actual files.

How to use it: Tap the box below and pick a .crate file from your Serato folder. It'll check every track against Apple Music right in your browser. Tap any result to open it in Apple Music. Tracks with no confident match get flagged so you know what's missing.

Feedback? If a match is wrong or a crate won't parse, open an issue on GitHub.
